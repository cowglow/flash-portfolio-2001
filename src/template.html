Rendered with Ruffle 0.7.1, the open-source Flash Player emulator; slide timings read from each file's timeline (scene = 49 frames, then gotoAndPlay). Two files, avs-001 and dbe-001, were authored at 61 fps, so their slides change about every 0.8 s.
